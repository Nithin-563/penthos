**Supervised Learning**  
- **Definition**: Uses labeled data (input-output pairs) to train models to predict outcomes. The model learns patterns from known examples.  
- **Example Task**: **Email Spam Classification** (e.g., classifying emails as "spam" or "not spam" based on content).  

**Unsupervised Learning**  
- **Definition**: Uses unlabeled data to find hidden patterns or structures without predefined labels. The goal is to discover intrinsic relationships.  
- **Example Task**: **Customer Segmentation** (e.g., grouping customers into clusters based on purchasing behavior without prior labels).  

**Key Difference**: Supervised learning requires explicit labels for training, while unsupervised learning works with raw, unlabeled data.
